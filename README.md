# SE Mini Project: Corporate Mental Wellness & Counseling Platform

Problem Statement #14, Healthcare and Telemedicine. Section B.

An anonymous counseling portal for a company. Employees can check their stress level, book a confidential session with a licensed therapist and follow their own wellness trend, and the employer never learns who used it.
